Our Story

Built on Plain Talk and Real Attention

Sterling & Chen Law Group was founded by Michael Sterling with a simple goal: give clients the same caliber of counsel as the country's largest firms, without losing the personal relationship along the way.

Since then, the firm has grown into a full-service practice covering business, estate planning, family, and real estate law — while holding on to the things that mattered from the start: clear communication, realistic advice, and attorneys who pick up the phone.

Every client meets directly with the attorney handling their matter. We believe that's the only way to give advice that actually fits your situation.
