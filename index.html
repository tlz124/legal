Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to a few things prospective clients often ask before reaching out.

Do you offer an initial consultation?

Yes. We offer an initial consultation so we can understand your situation and explain how we can help before you commit to anything.

What should I bring to my first meeting?

Any documents related to your matter — contracts, prior legal correspondence, court filings, or estate documents — along with a list of questions you'd like answered.

How long does a typical case take?

It depends on the matter. Straightforward contracts or estate plans can often be completed in a few weeks, while litigation and probate matters may take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline after reviewing your situation.

Do you work with clients outside of Phoenix?

We primarily serve clients throughout Arizona and can discuss out-of-state matters on a case-by-case basis.

How do I know which attorney is right for my case?

Reach out through our contact form or by phone, and we'll match you with the attorney whose practice area fits your needs.

How do I get started?

Submit the contact form or call our office. We'll follow up to schedule your consultation.

Ready to talk to an attorney?

Reach out today and we'll follow up to schedule your consultation.
